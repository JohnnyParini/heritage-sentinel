# Description
In this project, we are creating an artificial intelligence system that is capable of classifying damage to an artifact and establishing a sequence of restoration steps. It will have a damage classifier component that will determine the degree of damage an artifact has suffered, a restoration planner component that will establish a valid sequence of steps for repair based on prerequisites and damage level, and finally a provenance graph that traces the steps of repair.
